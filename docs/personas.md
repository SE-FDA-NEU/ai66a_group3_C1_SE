# Personas

These personas represent behaviour patterns in the anonymous survey. They are
not profiles of named individuals.

## Persona 1 - Frequent Explorer

### Context

This user watches movies at least twice a week and wants to find something new
without repeatedly scrolling through popular lists or random suggestions.

### Goal

Find a relevant movie quickly when there are many possible choices.

### Pain points

- Too many movies make it difficult to decide.
- Existing recommendations can feel unrelated to current interests.
- The user may know a preferred genre but still struggle to find a suitable
  title.

### Needs from the product

- Select favourite genres as a clear input to recommendations.
- Receive a small, relevant recommendation list with a reason for each match.
- Filter the current list by genre.
- Open a movie details page and continue exploring similar movies.

### Relevant stories

- S01 - Select favourite genres.
- S02 - View personalised recommendations.
- S03 - View movie details.
- S06 - Filter recommendations by genre.
- S07 - View similar movies.

### Evidence

- 4 of 8 respondents watched movies at least twice a week.
- Choice overload was reported by 6 of 8 respondents.
- Favourite genres, personalised recommendations, and genre filtering each
  scored 4.125 out of 5.

## Persona 2 - Occasional Undecided Viewer

### Context

This user watches movies infrequently and may not know what to search for when
opening a movie service. The user can give up, rewatch an old movie, or choose a
movie at random.

### Goal

Start exploring immediately and find a reasonable movie without needing a
complete preference profile.

### Pain points

- Not knowing where to start.
- Spending a long time selecting a movie.
- Receiving no suitable result after choosing a genre.

### Needs from the product

- See popular movies immediately when no preferences are available.
- Have a visible action to select one to five favourite genres.
- See popular alternatives and an option to edit preferences when no matching
  recommendation exists.
- Use the product without being required to rate movies.

### Relevant stories

- S01 - Select favourite genres.
- S02 - View personalised recommendations and no-match handling.
- S04 - Explore movies without preferences.
- S05a - Rate a movie as optional feedback.

### Evidence

- 4 of 8 respondents watched movies less than once per month.
- 5 of 8 respondents wanted both popular movies and genre selection for
  cold-start.
- 6 of 8 respondents preferred popular alternatives after no matching result.
- No respondent rated movies frequently.
